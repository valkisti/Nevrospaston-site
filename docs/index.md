## What is Digital Fabrication?

Digital Fabrication refers to the process of producing physical objects derectly from digital designs using computer-controlled tools and machines. It encompasses a wide range od techniques where digital data(such as 3D models or vector files) is transformed into physical items, often through sybtractve, additive, or transformative processes.
